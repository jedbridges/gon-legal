Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026. This policy covers the GON: Spin & Match app for iPhone ("GON").

The short version

GON does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

GON saves your best scores, the highest shape you reached, your remaining continues, your chosen skin and your sound setting on your iPhone only, using Apple's standard on-device storage. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, GON sends your scores to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. Apple handles that data under Apple's Privacy Policy. GON never sees your Apple ID or contact details.

Purchases

Continues and skins are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; GON only learns that a purchase succeeded so it can unlock the item. GON never receives your payment details.

Children

GON is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
